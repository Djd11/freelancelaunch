New here? What the words on this page mean
Shift — one of the three stages: Skill Acquisition (learn by rebuilding real projects), Mock Contract (fulfill a real brief), Send Proposals (bid on live jobs).
Gate — a checkpoint you pass to unlock the next shift. Gate A ends Skill Acquisition; Gate B ends the Mock Contract.
Copy-Work — rebuilding an existing high-performing project from a blank canvas, instead of watching videos.
Cluster — the group of real job postings your sprint is trained against (e.g. Email Automation).
Job Unlock Meter — how many real postings you've opened by finishing days. It's proof your skill is in demand.
