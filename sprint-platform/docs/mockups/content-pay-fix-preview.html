📝 Quiz — check before you pitch

3–4 Q per lesson: ≥60% recall, ≥20% application, ≥1 analysis. Answers explained & verified.

Q1 (recall) Which trigger starts an abandoned-cart flow?
A) Placed Order · B) Checkout Started ✓ · C) Subscribed to List
Answer: B) Checkout Started — fires when checkout begins; Placed Order fires after payment and misses the abandonment window.
Q2 (application) Your client wants the coupon only in the second email. Where do you place it?
A) Step 1 · B) Step 2 ✓ · C) Both steps
Answer: B) Step 2 — first email nudges; second incentivizes with 10% off, matching the rubric.
Q3 (analysis) A teammate used "Cart Value > $0" as trigger. Why will this misfire?
Answer: It fires for any cart creation, including browse carts that never entered checkout — noise vs intent. Checkout Started is intent-gated.
