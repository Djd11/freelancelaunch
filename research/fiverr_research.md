# Fiverr Research: What Successful Sellers Do Differently
> Compiled from r/Fiverr, r/freelance, and seller community analysis

## 1. Gig Ranking Factors
- **Title optimization is #1** — must match EXACTLY what buyers search
- **Order completion rate >90%** is critical for ranking
- **Response time <1 hour** with >90% response rate
- **Review velocity** matters — getting reviews quickly boosts rank more than total count
- **Gig image quality** — professional images get 2x clicks over amateur ones
- **Delivery time** — faster delivery = higher conversion rate and ranking boost

## 2. Gig Description That Converts
- **First 2 lines visible in search results** — must sell immediately
- **Format**: What → Why you → Proof → FAQ
- **Package structure**: Basic (essential) / Standard (popular) / Premium (comprehensive)
- **Pricing**: Basic = 70% of market, Standard = market rate, Premium = 130% of market
- **Gig extras**: Fast delivery, additional revisions, commercial use rights

## 3. Getting First 5 Reviews
- **Price 20-30% below market** for first 5 orders
- **Send 10+ buyer requests daily** — Fiverr's proposal equivalent
- **Over-deliver on every order** — add something unexpected
- **Ask for review** 2-3 days after delivery (not immediately)
- **Offer existing gig at 50% discount** to first 5 clients via custom offers
