# Contra Research: What Makes Freelancers Win
> Compiled from Contra community, user experiences, and platform analysis

## 1. How Contra's Matching Works
- **Portfolio-first matching** — Contra indexes portfolio items, not profiles
- **Skill tags** determine search visibility — use ALL relevant tags
- **No bidding/waiting** — clients send proposals to you when they find your portfolio
- **No commission** — you keep 100% of earnings
- **Discovery is the challenge** — you need to be found, not apply

## 2. Portfolio Presentation Best Practices
- **3-5 portfolio items minimum** — each with: Problem → Approach → Result
- **Show process, not just final work** — clients want to see HOW you think
- **Include measurable results** — "Increased conversion by 40%" beats "Did SEO work"
- **Self-initiated projects count** — build samples if you lack client work
- **Add context** — what was the challenge, timeline, tools used

## 3. Pricing on Commission-Free Platform
- **Price 15-20% higher than Upwork** — you keep everything
- **Typical rates**: $40-75/hr (vs $25-50/hr on Upwork for same work)
- **Project-based pricing** preferred over hourly
- **Transparent pricing** in profile builds trust
- **"Let's discuss"** option for complex projects

## 4. How to Get Discovered
- **Complete EVERY profile field** — incomplete profiles rank lower
- **Add availability status** — "Available now" gets priority
- **Link external work** — GitHub, Dribbble, Behance, LinkedIn all boost visibility
- **Share your Contra profile** on LinkedIn, Twitter, personal site
- **Apply to Contra's curated projects** — different from open marketplace
